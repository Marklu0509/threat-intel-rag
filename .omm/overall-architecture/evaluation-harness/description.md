Scripts and data that measure the pipeline: retrieval hit@k and MRR (scripts/evaluate.py), intent detection on separate dev/test sets (evaluate_intent.py), cited answers and refusals (evaluate_answers.py), faithfulness via an LLM judge and its agreement with human labels (judge_faithfulness.py, judge_agreement.py), prompt-injection and poisoning attacks on isolated index copies (evaluate_attacks.py), and answer language (check_language.py). Questions in eval/*.jsonl, results in eval/results/, summary in eval/RESULTS.md. build_demo_data.py turns recorded runs into site/demo-data.json.
